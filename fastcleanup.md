# Privacy Policy

**Fast Cleanup: Storage Cleaner** is a product of **OneBoom Media**. We take your privacy protection very seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information.

---

## 1. Data Access and Usage

Depending on the specific features you use, we may require access to certain data after you grant explicit permission:

- **Photo Library:** We access photos and videos **solely** to scan for duplicates and large files to help you optimize storage. We do not upload them.
- **Contacts:** We access your contact list **only** to identify and merge duplicate entries.
- **Biometric Data:** We use system-level Face ID / Touch ID for the "Private Vault" feature. We **do not** store or access your actual biometric data; verification happens locally on your device.

## 2. On-Device Processing & Security

- **Local Processing:** Most core functions, such as photo scanning and organization, are performed **locally on your device**.
- **Data Protection:** We utilize Apple's Data Protection technology to ensure that all sensitive data remains encrypted and secure.
- **No Server Uploads:** We **do not upload** your private photos, contacts, or other personal files to our servers. Your data stays on your device.

## 3. Information Sharing

- We **do not** sell, rent, or disclose your personal information to any third party.
- Your data is **never** used for advertising or data harvesting purposes.

## 4. Data Security

We implement strict security measures and utilize the latest iOS security frameworks to ensure your information is not accessed, used, or disclosed unlawfully.
