    <p>We collect two types of information:</p>

    <h3>A. User-Generated Content (Stored Locally & Offline)</h3>
    <p>The following data is <strong>stored locally on your device</strong> to ensure your privacy and offline accessibility. It is not transmitted to our servers unless you explicitly perform a backup action:</p>
    <ul>
        <li>Game progress, high scores, and brain training history</li>
        <li>Psychological test results, personality assessments, and cognitive scores</li>
        <li>Personal notes or journals</li>
        <li><strong>Offline Data Independence:</strong> All the above data remains accessible even without an internet connection, ensuring your personal insights stay with you.</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>B. Usage & Technical Data (Collected by Third Parties)</h3>
    <p>To improve app stability, analyze marketing effectiveness, and enhance user experience, we use third-party services that may collect:</p>
    <ul>
        <li><strong>Device Information:</strong> Device model, OS version, and unique device identifiers (e.g., IDFV).</li>
        <li><strong>Advertising Identifiers (IDFA):</strong> If you grant permission via the App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt, we may collect your IDFA for advertisement attribution (to understand how you found our app).</li>
        <li><strong>Usage Data:</strong> Launch times, session duration, and feature interaction.</li>
        <li><strong>Crash Logs:</strong> Information about app crashes and system state at the time of error.</li>
    </ul>

    <h2>3. How We Use Your Information</h2>
    <p><strong>Local Data:</strong> To provide the core functionality of the app, such as saving your game levels and generating personal insights locally.</p>
    <p><strong>Technical Data:</strong></p>
    <ul>
        <li>To analyze app performance and fix bugs (via Crashlytics).</li>
        <li>To understand user engagement trends (via Firebase Analytics).</li>
        <li>To measure the effectiveness of our advertising campaigns (via Meta/Facebook SDK).</li>
    </ul>

    <ul>
        <li>Subscription status and purchase history are managed by Apple's StoreKit framework.</li>
        <li>We only receive information about your subscription status (active/inactive) to enable premium features (and offline access).</li>
        <li>We do not have access to your payment details, credit card information, or billing address.</li>
    </ul>

    <p>You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time through your Apple ID account settings. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period.</p>

    <p>Refund requests for subscriptions must be processed through Apple's refund policy. Please contact Apple Support for subscription refunds.</p>
