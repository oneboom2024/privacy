# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: January 8, 2026**

---

## 1. Introduction

BrainSPA ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data when you use our mobile application.

## 2. Information We Collect

We collect two types of information:

### A. User-Generated Content (Stored Locally)

The following data is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to our servers unless you explicitly perform a backup action (if available):

* Game progress and high scores
* Psychological test results and assessments
* Personal notes or journals

### B. Usage & Technical Data (Collected by Third Parties)

To improve app stability and user experience, we use third-party services that may collect:

* **Device Information:** Device model, OS version, and unique device identifiers (e.g., IDFV).
* **Usage Data:** Launch times, session duration, and feature interaction.
* **Crash Logs:** Information about app crashes and system state at the time of error.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

**Local Data:** To provide the core functionality of the app, such as saving your game levels and generating personal insights.

**Technical Data:** To analyze app performance, fix bugs (via Crashlytics), and understand user engagement trends (via Firebase Analytics).

## 4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services. These providers have their own privacy policies addressing how they use such information:

**Google Firebase (Analytics & Crashlytics)**

We use Firebase to analyze how users interact with BrainSPA and to monitor system stability. Firebase may collect your IP address and device identifiers.

[Firebase Privacy Policy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)

## 5. Data Storage & Security

**Personal Data:** Your sensitive personal data (such as mental health test results) remains on your device within the app's sandbox environment using iOS standard storage (UserDefaults, Core Data).

**Data Transfer:** While your personal content remains local, anonymous usage statistics are processed by Firebase servers to help us improve the App.

## 6. Your Rights & Opt-Out

You have the right to:

* Access or delete your local data by uninstalling the app.
* **Opt-out of Analytics:** You can disable tracking in your iOS device settings (Settings > Privacy > Tracking) or through the app's settings menu if available.

## 7. Children's Privacy

BrainSPA is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date on this page.
