    <section>
      <h2>About this policy</h2>
      <p>
        This Privacy Policy explains what information Eden collects, how we use
        and share it, and the choices available to you when you use the app.
      </p>
    </section>

    <section>
      <h2>Information you provide</h2>
      <p>
        When you create an account or edit your profile, we may receive your
        account identifier, email address, display name, and profile image. We
        also process content you choose to create, including community prayer
        requests, Bible favorites, highlights, notes, reading history,
        devotional check-ins, app preferences, support messages, and prompts
        submitted to AI features.
      </p>
    </section>

    <section>
      <h2>App activity, analytics, and diagnostics</h2>
      <p>
        Eden uses Google Firebase and Meta app-event tools to understand app
        performance, improve features, and measure subscription and advertising
        results. Depending on the event and provider, this data may include
        screens viewed; onboarding progress; sign-in method; interactions with
        Bible, Explain, Chat with Eden, Listening, Community, sharing, and other
        features; Bible search terms; content or topic identifiers; paywall
        source; selected plan; checkout status; and trial, subscription, or
        purchase status.
      </p>

      <p>
        Technical data may include app version, device model, operating system,
        language, approximate region derived from an IP address, installation or
        device identifiers, crash logs, stack traces, and diagnostics.
      </p>

      <p>
        Eden does not intentionally send the text of AI conversations, notes, or
        community prayer requests to Firebase or Meta as analytics event
        parameters.
      </p>
    </section>

    <section>
      <h2>Tracking and advertising identifiers</h2>
      <p>
        Eden asks for permission through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency
        framework before using data to track you across apps or websites owned
        by other companies.
      </p>

      <p>
        If you allow tracking, Meta and related measurement services may receive
        an advertising identifier and app-event data for advertising attribution
        and measurement.
      </p>

      <p>
        If you deny permission, Eden will not access the advertising identifier
        for cross-app tracking; limited first-party analytics, fraud prevention,
        and crash diagnostics may still operate.
      </p>

      <p>
        You can change this permission at any time in
        <strong>iOS Settings &gt; Privacy &amp; Security &gt; Tracking</strong>.
      </p>
    </section>

    <section>
      <h2>How we use information</h2>
      <p>
        We use information to provide and personalize Bible reading, prayer,
        audio, community, reminders, account synchronization, AI responses,
        customer support, subscription access, security, troubleshooting,
        analytics, and product improvement.
      </p>

      <p>
        We may also use information to prevent abuse and enforce community
        safety.
      </p>
    </section>

    <section>
      <h2>Community content</h2>
      <p>
        Prayer requests you publish are visible to other Eden users together
        with your display name and profile image or initial. Other users may
        interact with that content.
      </p>

      <p>
        Do not publish sensitive personal information that you do not want
        others to see.
      </p>
    </section>

    <section>
      <h2>AI features</h2>
      <p>
        When you use Explain or Chat with Eden, your prompt, selected Scripture,
        and relevant recent conversation context are sent through Eden’s server
        infrastructure to Google Gemini to generate a response.
      </p>

      <p>
        AI chat history is stored on your device; generated Bible explanations
        may be cached on our servers so the same passage can be served more
        efficiently.
      </p>

      <p>
        Do not include confidential or highly sensitive personal information in
        AI prompts.
      </p>
    </section>

    <section>
      <h2>Subscriptions and purchases</h2>
      <p>
        Subscriptions and free trials are processed by Apple through the App
        Store. Apple receives and processes your payment information; Eden does
        not receive your full payment-card details.
      </p>

      <p>
        To provide subscription access and measure purchase performance, Eden
        and its analytics providers may process the selected product and plan,
        product identifier, price and currency in U.S. dollars, trial or
        subscription status, checkout result, and an App Store transaction or
        order identifier after a verified purchase.
      </p>

      <p>
        You can manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple ID subscription
        settings.
      </p>
    </section>

    <section>
      <h2>Service providers and sharing</h2>
      <p>
        We share information only as needed to operate Eden, comply with law,
        protect users, complete transactions, or perform the purposes described
        above.
      </p>

      <p>Our service providers include:</p>

      <ul>
        <li>
          <strong>Supabase</strong> for authentication, databases, file storage,
          and server functions
        </li>
        <li>
          <strong>Google Firebase</strong> for analytics and crash diagnostics
        </li>
        <li>
          <strong>Meta</strong> for app-event measurement and advertising
          attribution
        </li>
        <li>
          <strong>Google Gemini</strong> for AI responses
        </li>
        <li>
          <strong>Apple</strong> for Sign in with Apple, App Store purchases,
          and operating-system services
        </li>
      </ul>

      <p>
        These providers may process data under their own terms and privacy
        commitments. We do not sell your personal information.
      </p>
    </section>

    <section>
      <h2>Storage and retention</h2>
      <p>
        Some preferences, reminders, AI chat history, and listening progress are
        stored locally on your device.
      </p>

      <p>
        If you sign in, profile information, community posts, devotional
        check-ins, favorites, highlights, notes, and reading history may be
        stored with Supabase and synchronized across devices.
      </p>

      <p>
        We retain information while it is needed to provide Eden, meet legal
        obligations, resolve disputes, maintain security, and enforce our
        agreements. Retention periods may vary by data type and service provider.
      </p>
    </section>

    <section>
      <h2>Your choices and deletion</h2>
      <p>
        You can change app and notification preferences in Eden or iOS Settings,
        edit your profile, remove content you created, sign out, or delete your
        account from Settings.
      </p>

      <p>
        Account deletion removes your account, profile, uploaded avatar,
        community posts, and associated synced content, except where limited
        retention is required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or backup
        purposes.
      </p>

      <p>
        Deleting Eden from your device removes locally stored app data according
        to iOS behavior but does not by itself delete an Eden account.
      </p>
    </section>

    <section>
      <h2>Security and international processing</h2>

      <p>
