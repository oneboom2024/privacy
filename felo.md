    <h1>Felo Privacy Policy</h1>
    <p><strong>Felo</strong> takes your privacy protection very seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information.</p>

    <h3>1. Information Collection</h3>
    <p><strong>1.1 Types of information we collect:</strong></p>
    <ul>
        <li><strong>Menstrual cycle data</strong> (period start date, end date, etc.)</li>
        <li><strong>Health record data</strong> (mood, pain level, discharge, sexual activity, digestion, etc.)</li>
        <li><strong>Cycle settings</strong> (average cycle length, period length)</li>
        <li><strong>Reminder settings and preferences</strong></li>
        <li><strong>App technical usage data</strong> (for service improvement and crash reporting)</li>
    </ul>
    <p><strong>1.2 We do NOT collect:</strong></p>
    <ul>
        <li>Your personal identification information (name, ID number, etc.)</li>
        <li>Your contact information (phone number, home address, etc.)</li>
        <li>Your <strong>precise GPS</strong> location information</li>
        <li>Your photos or other media files</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>2. Information Use</h3>
    <p><strong>2.1 We use collected information to:</strong></p>
    <ul>
        <li>Provide cycle prediction and analysis features</li>
        <li>Generate personalized reminder notifications</li>
        <li>Display health trends and statistics</li>
        <li>Improve app functionality, stability, and user experience</li>
    </ul>
    <p><strong>2.2 We do NOT:</strong></p>
    <ul>
        <li>Sell your data to third parties</li>
        <li>Use your <strong>personal health data</strong> for advertising purposes</li>
        <li>Share your <strong>personal health data</strong> without your consent</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>3. Data Storage and Security</h3>
    <p><strong>3.1 Data Storage:</strong></p>
    <ul>
        <li><strong>Local Storage First:</strong> All your <strong>Personal Health Data</strong> (including cycle records and symptoms) is stored locally on your device only.</li>
        <li><strong>Technology:</strong> We use the SwiftData framework for secure local data management.</li>
        <li><strong>No Health Data Upload:</strong> Your <strong>Personal Health Data</strong> is <strong>not</strong> uploaded to any of our servers. (Please note that anonymous technical data is processed by third-party services as strictly described in Section 7).</li>
    </ul>
    <p><strong>3.2 Security Measures:</strong></p>
    <ul>
        <li>Support for Face ID / Touch ID biometric protection</li>
        <li>Encrypted data storage</li>
        <li>App lock feature to protect privacy</li>
    </ul>
    <p><strong>3.3 Data Backup:</strong></p>
    <ul>
        <li>If your device has iCloud backup enabled, your app data may be included in your personal iCloud backups designated by Apple.</li>
        <li>You can manage iCloud backups through your device settings.</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>4. Notification Permission</h3>
    <p>4.1 To provide reminder features, we need access to your notification permission.</p>
    <p>4.2 You can turn off notification permission in system settings at any time.</p>
    <p>4.3 We provide a <strong>Privacy Mode</strong> to hide sensitive words in notification content.</p>

    <h3>5. Data Export</h3>
    <p>5.1 You can export your data through the "Export Health Report" feature.</p>
    <p>5.2 Exported data is provided in text format (CSV/PDF), which you can save or share yourself.</p>

    <h3>6. Third-Party Services</h3>
    <p>6.1 This App integrates <strong>Firebase Analytics</strong> and <strong>Firebase Crashlytics</strong> for app analytics and crash reporting. For detailed information, please refer to the "7. Third-Party SDKs" section.</p>
    <p>6.2 This App will <strong>not</strong> sell or share your <strong>Personal Health Data</strong> to third parties for advertising or other commercial purposes.</p>

    <h3>7. Third-Party SDKs</h3>
    <p>To improve app quality and user experience, we have integrated the following third-party SDKs provided by Google.</p>
    
    <p><strong>7.1 Firebase Analytics</strong> (Google Firebase Analytics Service)</p>
    <ul>
        <li><strong>Purpose:</strong> Collect app usage statistics to analyze user engagement and improve features.</li>
        <li><strong>Information Collected:</strong> App usage events (e.g., feature clicks, screen views), <strong>Device Identifiers (such as IDFV/Instance ID)</strong>, IP addresses, and other anonymous statistical data.</li>
        <li><strong>Data Usage:</strong> Used only for app analysis and improvement.</li>
        <li><strong>Privacy Note:</strong> Firebase Analytics does <strong>not</strong> have access to your menstrual cycle data or health records.</li>
        <li><strong>Privacy Policy:</strong> <a href="https://policies.google.com/privacy" target="_blank">https://policies.google.com/privacy</a></li>
    </ul>

    <p><strong>7.2 Firebase Crashlytics</strong> (Google Firebase Crash Reporting Service)</p>
    <ul>
        <li><strong>Purpose:</strong> Collect app crash and error information to help fix bugs and improve stability.</li>
        <li><strong>Information Collected:</strong> Crash logs, device state information (device model, OS version, free disk space), and installation UUIDs.</li>
        <li><strong>Data Usage:</strong> Used only for diagnosing and fixing app issues.</li>
        <li><strong>Privacy Note:</strong> Does not collect your personal identification information or health data.</li>
        <li><strong>Privacy Policy:</strong> <a href="https://policies.google.com/privacy" target="_blank">https://policies.google.com/privacy</a></li>
    </ul>

    <p><strong>7.3 Important Clarification:</strong></p>
    <ul>
        <li>The above third-party SDKs <strong>DO NOT</strong> collect your menstrual cycle data, specific health records, or other sensitive personal information.</li>
        <li>Your Personal Health Data remains stored only locally on your device.</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>8. Children's Privacy</h3>
    <p>8.1 This App is intended for users aged 18 and above.</p>
    <p>8.2 We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 18.</p>

    <h3>9. Your Rights</h3>
    <p><strong>9.1 You have the right to:</strong></p>
    <ul>
        <li>View, modify, or delete your data at any time within the app.</li>
        <li>Export your health data.</li>
        <li>Turn off notification features.</li>
        <li>Uninstall the App (uninstalling will permanently delete all local data).</li>
    </ul>
    <p><strong>9.2 Data Deletion:</strong></p>
    <ul>
        <li>Since data is stored locally, you can delete all data by simply uninstalling the App.</li>
        <li><strong>Warning:</strong> Deleted data cannot be recovered; please proceed with caution.</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>10. Privacy Mode</h3>
    <p>10.1 We provide a Privacy Mode feature to hide sensitive words (e.g., "Period", "Menstruation") in push notifications.</p>
    <p>10.2 When Privacy Mode is enabled, notification messages will use discreet expressions.</p>

    <p>11.2 Updated policies will be published in the App settings.</p>
    <p>11.3 Continued use of the App constitutes acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.</p>
