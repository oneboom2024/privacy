Felo takes your privacy protection very seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information.

1. Information Collection
1.1 Types of information we collect:
    - Menstrual cycle data (period start date, end date, etc.)
    - Health record data (mood, pain level, discharge, sexual activity, digestion, etc.)
    - Cycle settings (average cycle length, period length)
    - Reminder settings and preferences
    - App usage data (for service improvement)

1.2 We do not collect:
    - Your personal identification information (name, ID number, etc.)
    - Your contact information (phone, email, etc.)
    - Your device location information
    - Your photos or other media files

2. Information Use
2.1 We use collected information to:
    - Provide cycle prediction and analysis features
    - Generate personalized reminder notifications
    - Display health trends and statistics
    - Improve app functionality and user experience

2.2 We do not:
    - Sell your data to third parties
    - Use your data for advertising purposes
    - Share your personal health data without your consent

3. Data Storage and Security
3.1 Data Storage:
    - All your data is stored locally on your device only
    - We use SwiftData framework for local data management
    - Data is not uploaded to any server

3.2 Security Measures:
    - Support for Face ID / Touch ID biometric protection
    - Encrypted data storage
    - App lock feature to protect privacy

3.3 Data Backup:
    - If your device has iCloud backup enabled, data may be included in iCloud backups
    - You can manage iCloud backups through device settings

4. Notification Permission
4.1 To provide reminder features, we need access to your notification permission.
4.2 You can turn off notification permission in system settings at any time.
4.3 We provide privacy mode to hide sensitive words in notification content.

5. Data Export
5.1 You can export your data through the "Export Health Report" feature.
5.2 Exported data is provided in text format, which you can save or share yourself.

6. Third-Party Services
6.1 This App does not integrate any third-party analytics or advertising services.
6.2 This App does not collect or share any data with third parties.

7. Children's Privacy
7.1 This App is intended for users aged 18 and above.
7.2 We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Your Rights
8.1 You have the right to:
    - View, modify, or delete your data at any time
    - Export your data
    - Turn off notification features
    - Uninstall the App (uninstalling will delete all local data)

8.2 Data Deletion:
    - You can delete all data by uninstalling the App
    - Deleted data cannot be recovered, please proceed with caution

9. Privacy Mode
9.1 We provide privacy mode feature to hide sensitive words in notifications.
9.2 When privacy mode is enabled, notification messages will use discreet expressions.

10. Policy Updates
10.1 We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.
10.2 Updated policies will be published in the App.
10.3 Continued use of the App constitutes acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.
